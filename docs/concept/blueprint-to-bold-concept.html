A-00 · The idea

From blueprintto bold.

Faiza's career started in architecture, so her website starts as an architect's drawing. Each section arrives as fine blue linework and settles into full color as you scroll. The site tells her story through how it behaves, with the plan coming first and the bold move after it.

  • LeadershipHeavy, wide headlines, a clear route through the page, and her framework presented as a method with four named moves.
  • MentoringFaiza speaks in first person, her words get a warm serif of their own, and the page keeps asking the reader questions.
  • Forward thinkingEvery section points to the next step, and the page ends on Design Your Next Move with a tool for doing exactly that.

A-04

Signature details

Four things a visitor should remember the site by.

Shift the lens

A switch in the menu bar flips the whole site between blueprint and bold. Her closing line, “Just shift the lens,” becomes something visitors can do.

Drawn, then built

Sections arrive as blue linework and settle into full color as they scroll into view, while headlines thicken from hairline to heavy. Visitors who turn off motion see the finished page straight away.

Architect's annotations

Dimension lines, margin notes and section markers carry the labels and navigation. Her architecture training shows on every page without a paragraph to explain it.

The B.O.L.D monogram

The letters sit inside a drawing frame and the B breaks through its edge, which is Break the Frame as a mark. It works as the logo, the favicon and a social avatar.
